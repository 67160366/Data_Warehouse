# Business Context

3Eyes is presented as a wearable QC screening aid for inspecting hex-head, zinc-plated fasteners. The educational business narrative explores whether an additional screening step could reduce customer escapes while managing false alarms, uncertain decisions, labor time, and device costs.

No real model performance is asserted. Defect mix, recall, operating costs, and payback figures in this repository are simulated assumptions for a Data Warehouse exercise.
